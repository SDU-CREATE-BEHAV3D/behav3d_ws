Screen assumptions and tool calibration

Base support: at least 6 of 9 rays within ±3 mm of the nominal bottom plane. Candidate centres: at least 32 mm apart (36 mm nominal proxy minus 4 mm assumed overlap). Tool: 2 mm surface margin, 2 mm TCP exclusion, 3 mm path sampling. These are explicit experimental thresholds; the 0° control is evaluated with the same assumptions.

The tool check samples CAD vertices and face centroids. It does not certify continuous mesh collision, inside/outside containment, wrist/arm clearance or unobserved space. A green candidate still needs the remaining checks.

Contact study interpretation

The contact search tests fixed height/side locations on the recorded inner face of the right wall. Correction is outward (+X), toward that point’s pre-edit location. The paddle and existing extruder are compared on the same free approach, which ends 6 mm before nominal contact.

Contact search, pushing and post-contact retreat are not collision-cleared or mechanically simulated. A point reaching its former location does not establish that the whole wall returns to shape. Force, speed, compliance, mount calibration and abort conditions need physical validation before a robot trajectory can be produced.
