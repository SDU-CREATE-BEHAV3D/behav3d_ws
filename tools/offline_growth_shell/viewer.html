Comparison method and remaining checks

Archived C19 targets retain their recorded positions and directions. They are rescored on each edited scan. The unchanged-goal and revised-goal batches use the same current generation and filtering rules. Their comparison isolates the effect of the growth-surface revision. Original archived directions were rounded in the recording.

Batch rows use a one-to-one spatial association within 36 mm. Unmatched targets remain listed. This is a comparison convention, not bead identity or a permitted motion distance. Local alternatives each replace only C19:T7 with the other six archived endpoints reserved; those alternatives are never a combined batch. Batch spacing is checked against the peers in each respective batch.

Positions are in ur20_base_link; displayed coordinates are in mm. Exported coordinates are in metres, with full numerical precision and unit tool Z directions. A direction is not a complete robot pose. The 36 × 16 mm bead proxy is borrowed from C18 because C19 dimensions were not recorded.

The fit uses Agent007 pairs marked eligible in the upper wall. Controlled edits move observed cap proxies and the recorded scan; the detector is not rerun. Each revised field preserves vertex identities, topology, Z coordinates and scalar ordering. The planner recomputes gradients and its mesh/frontier intersections on the new geometry. The numerical collar below the frontier does not move already printed material.
