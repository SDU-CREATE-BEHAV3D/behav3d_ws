How to interpret this test

  • Cases were chosen by construction stage and recorded findings before scoring. This is a diagnostic sample from one recording, not a representative accuracy benchmark.
  • Zero additions leaves an inspection open when the current area-based model cannot identify a useful view.
  • Additional views predict geometric access. Their effects on raw depth, reconstruction, and subsequent printing candidates have not been tested with new captures.
  • Registration and material identity remain unverified. Camera/tool assumptions and conservative neighbor envelopes affect the results.
  • A DDS status is provisional recorded evidence; missing or extra does not establish a physical defect.
